# User Interview — PA App

- **Who:** Pyone Cho
- **When:** 2026-07-25
- **How:** In person — ~20 min

## What they do today (without your project)

Pyone uses a mix of Google Calendar for scheduling, Google Keep for quick notes, a physical notebook for journaling, and separate health/fitness apps for workouts and habits. Expenses are tracked manually in a spreadsheet. There's no single place where tasks, habits, journaling, expenses, and goals live together — switching between 5+ tools every day feels scattered.

## What they liked

- "I like that everything is in one app — tasks, habits, journal, expenses. I don't need 5 different things open."
- "The dashboard is useful for a quick overview of my day. The weather widget is a nice touch."
- "The sidebar navigation makes sense — I can jump between sections without going back to a home page."
- "The productivity score is motivating. Seeing 80/100 makes me want to fill the rest."
- "Quick Capture with Ctrl+K is convenient for adding tasks without navigating away."
- "The dark mode looks clean and modern."

## What confused them / what's missing

- "I wasn't sure what 'Kanban' meant at first. Maybe a tooltip or rename would help."
- "The Mood Tracker on the dashboard — I didn't notice it until you pointed it out. It blends in with everything else."
- "I expected the Habits section in the sidebar to go to a dedicated page, but there's no page for it."
- "The Focus Timer — I don't know what 25 minutes is for. Is it a Pomodoro? Can I change the duration?"
- "Ambient Sounds dropdown is empty. I clicked it and nothing happened."
- "I want to see a weekly view of my habits, not just a list."
- "Expenses doesn't have a monthly budget feature — I'd like to set a limit and see when I'm close."
- "The calendar shows dots for tasks but I can't create a task directly from the calendar view."

## What would make them actually use it

- "A daily or weekly habit view so I can check things off through the week."
- "Notifications or reminders for tasks and habits — I forget to open the app."
- "A proper month-by-month expense breakdown with charts."
- "More customization on the dashboard — let me choose which widgets to show."
- "If it had a mobile app, I'd use it daily. Opening a browser on my phone feels heavy."

## What they'd pay / recommend

- Would recommend to friends who also juggle multiple productivity tools.
- Sees potential as a paid app if the mobile experience and notifications were added.
- The all-in-one approach is the strongest selling point vs. using separate tools.

## What I'll change (next steps)

- [ ] Rename "Kanban" to "Board" or add a tooltip explaining kanban view
- [ ] Highlight Mood Tracker more prominently or move it to a dedicated section
- [ ] Either create Habits page or remove from sidebar and keep as dashboard widget
- [ ] Add configurable Focus Timer duration and Pomodoro description
- [ ] Wire up Ambient Sounds with real data or remove the placeholder
- [ ] Add habit weekly view / checklist
- [ ] Add monthly budget tracking and spending charts to Expenses
